# Privacy Policy (DRAFT — not legal advice)

CohortOS processes coaching-centre student and staff data to provide attendance, fees, exams, and related desk features.

- **Children’s data:** Many students are minors. Centres act as data controllers for their students; CohortOS is a processor for hosted deployments.
- **Local-first:** Default offline desk keeps data on the centre’s machine.
- **Cloud AI:** Disabled by default. When enabled by the centre owner, prompts may be sent to the configured provider; PII redaction is applied where implemented.
- **Retention:** Centres can export and delete data from Settings → Backup.
- **Contact:** support@cohortos.app

This draft is **not legal advice**. Have counsel review before production use in Bangladesh under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026.
